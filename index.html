Hi!, You want to know about me? Interesting... I’m Nikhil, a student and a professional enthusiast of trying things I probably have no business trying. I’ve experimented with everything from Blender and websites to apps, electronics, game developing, content creation, digital products, cooking, drawing, football, boxing, Taekwondo, fitness, music, and about a hundred other things that somehow seemed like a good idea at the time. I’ve never really been the “master of one thing” type. I’m more interested in discovering what I enjoy by actually doing it. I want to travel the world, taste ridiculous amounts of food, learn instruments, make things, break things, and collect experiences instead of spending my life wondering what something might have been like. I’m still figuring out what I want to get seriously good at, but for now, I’m enjoying being curious, building projects, and seeing where all these random interests eventually take me. Somewhere in all that, I’m hoping to find a few things worth mastering.

Graybit

A Gumroad store selling AI-generated prompt packs for stylized images.

One time there was a trend going on where people were trying image generation tool and making their cool images with different aesthetics , background , clothes , scenes and much more using a simple prompts so I thought why not setting up a digital store type where people can buy the prompts pack and use them at that time I jumped in without thinking much about marketing — just made the store and uploaded a few packs. I never sold anything, but I learned that selling digital products takes real marketing and targeting the right audience, not just uploading and hoping people find you.
